Bálint Mazzag

Biostatistician | Data Scientist
Budapest, HU

I'm a biostatistician working on causal inference with real-world health data, with a focus on Bayesian methods. I'm the statistician on HF‑EPI‑HUN, Hungary's 15-year nationwide heart failure study (European Journal of Heart Failure, 2026), and have worked on nationwide cohort studies in osteoporosis (Journal of Bone and Mineral Research, 2026) and paediatric ADHD. On the side I build TidyTransit, a real-time forecasting pipeline for Budapest transit data.
